Stage 1

Whole-song analysis

The visualizer's live listener is causal — it only ever sees the last couple of seconds, so it can chase a drop but never build toward one. This runs the whole track through an offline pass first: beat grid, downbeats, sections, drops, and separated instrument envelopes, all with timestamps. Nothing is uploaded; the analysis runs in your browser. Play with Click on to check the grid by ear — if the clicks sit in the pocket, the timeline is right.
